# Garbled text and `stream_output`

**Status:** Done

Builds [specs/agent.md](../specs/agent.md) "Garbled text" and the delivery modes of "Output: tags in the model's text", [specs/config.md](../specs/config.md) `stream_output` and [specs/instrumentation.md](../specs/instrumentation.md) `garbled_attempts`.

**Why.** A live run on `openai/gpt-oss-120b` (Hugging Face Hub, Cerebras) degenerated inside its output tag: a line of ellipses, blank space and stray `</`, then a restart, never closed. All of it was spoken, and kept twice for every later prompt: in the assistant-text record and in the output's World entry. No guard ran, because a response that has started an output is not guarded. The next answers degenerated the same way. Whether the garbage in history kept the model degenerating is not proven (the prompt was not kept), but nothing the model cannot use should stay in its context.

## Steps

1. **The check** (`wica.guards`): `find_garbled(text, *, truncated, tail_only)`. Filler share (ellipses, runs of dots and stray `</` counted as whole runs, gaps inside a line, runs of blank lines; indentation removed) over any 200-character window once the text has 80 characters, above 0.5; a complete text with no letter or digit that is mostly filler, whatever its length (a second reproduction: a whole `<say>` of `……………… ... ... ...</...>`; a short held output is judged whole when it ends); and a loop: a response cut at its cap whose last 400 characters compress below 0.2. The thresholds are constants. Checked against the 660 texts of the recorded live and benchmark reports: none flagged (highest filler share 0.07); the incident is flagged from its first 80 characters.
2. **The default guard `garbled_text`**, after `invalid_args`, over the outputs' bodies and the private text, as a reminder. `ModelResponse` gains `outputs` and `private_text` (the parsed text).
3. **`AgentConfig.stream_output`** (default `true`), loaded and validated like the other fields. With `false`, outputs are held while the response streams and opened, in order, with their whole bodies once the guards accepted the response.
4. **One voice at the first words.** The cut of an earlier response's outputs moves from `_open_output` to the parser's first-words callback, so it does not wait for a held output.
5. **Streaming with the check.** While `garbled_text` is installed: an output is held until 80 characters (or its end) and checked; the check runs on each piece after that; a response whose output started is checked whole at the end (the loop); garbled private text beside clean outputs is left out of the record. A trip in the stream raises from the parser's callback, ends the model stream (closed explicitly) and rejects the attempt; with an output started, the response is retracted: outputs cancelled, no assistant record, entries `failed` with no text. Held pieces are handed over as they arrived.
6. **Instrumentation.** `ResponseRejection.output_started`, `ReactionTrace.garbled_attempts`; a WARNING per garbled attempt.
7. **Docs.** The configuration reference (`stream_output`), the API reference (one voice, delivery, the fourth guard, the garbled-text paragraph, the new fields), the latency guide (the 80-character wait, `garbled_attempts`).
8. **On top of the tool-call output** ([202611172011_output-as-tool.md](202611172011_output-as-tool.md)). `ModelResponse.outputs` holds each output tool call's `text` in tool mode, so `garbled_text` catches a garbled call before anything is said (no hold-back or retraction there: the output waits for the response). `stream_output` becomes `bool | None`: unset is the mode's default (streamed with the tag, held with the tool call), `true` with `as_tool=True` raises. Tool-mode delivery cuts the earlier output (one voice) through the same step as the parser's first words. `combine` hides the response when any rejecting guard hides it, so garbage failing `output_command_required` too is not replayed.

## Tests

- `tests/test_guards.py`: the incident flagged (whole and from its first 80 characters); code, JSON, a table, emoji, numbers, French, Japanese, a countdown, hesitations and a song not flagged; a loop flagged only when cut; nothing under 80 characters; garbage after a clean start found by the full check but not by the tail-only one; the guard over outputs, private text and a hand-built response.
- `tests/test_agent.py` ("Garbled text", "stream_output false"): a garbled start never delivered, the stream stopped part-way, the retry spoken, only it in history; an output held back until 80 characters, its pieces kept, a short one delivered at its end; garbage after a clean start retracted (the output cancelled, its entry `failed` with no text, the next prompt free of it); a loop retracted at the end; garbled private text left out; out of retries nothing said or recorded; without the guard the garbage delivered as before; with `stream_output: false` an unknown Command beside a `<say>` rejected before anything is said, whole outputs keeping their pieces and order, and the earlier output cut at the new response's first words before the guards run.
- `tests/test_config.py`: `stream_output` default (unset), parsing and validation.
- Tool mode (`tests/test_agent.py`): a garbled call never said and retried with a reminder (`garbled_attempts` counted); garbled free text with no call hidden although `output_command_required` would show it; `stream_output: true` rejected with `as_tool=True`, `false` accepted. `tests/test_guards.py`: `combine` hides when any rejection hides.
